I'm a Software Engineering student, currently deep in coursework, competitive programming, and side projects that scratch an itch I actually care about — mostly backend systems and, lately, agri-tech for Bangla-speaking farmers.

        
My day-to-day lives somewhere between a Codeforces tab, a half-finished API, and a notebook of half-drawn UML diagrams. I like problems that have a clean, correct answer and the discipline it takes to find it — which is probably why competitive programming stuck with me.

        
Outside of pure code, I'm interested in how software can solve very local, very real problems — like giving smallholder farmers AI-backed crop advice in their own language.
